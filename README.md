Astra

Anonymous Reporting Platform for Social, Public & Workplace Issues

Astra is a web-based anonymous reporting platform that allows people to report social, public, and workplace issues without revealing their identity.

The platform is designed to give individuals a safe and accessible way to raise concerns while keeping their personal identity private. Users can submit reports, provide supporting information, track their reports, and communicate through the platform without directly exposing their identity.

🌐 What is Astra?

Many people experience problems in society, public places, educational institutions, and workplaces but hesitate to report them because they are concerned about:

Revealing their identity

Workplace consequences

Social pressure

Lack of a simple reporting system

Not knowing where or how to report an issue

Astra provides a centralized platform where users can submit complaints anonymously and track their progress.

Example Issues

Astra can be used to report issues such as:

Workplace harassment or misconduct

Ragging and bullying

Public safety concerns

Civic and infrastructure issues

Social issues

Other incidents that users believe should be brought to attention
 Key Features
